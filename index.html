    Privacy Policy & Patient Data Protection Notice

    Effective Date: September 26, 2026 | Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Principles

    1. Ephemeral Local-First Data Architecture

    MediBot Clinical Portal is engineered on a privacy-by-default architecture. By default, your symptom queries, pre-existing conditions, medication lists, and session history are stored strictly within your local browser session (sessionStorage) and are never transmitted to third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

    2. Granular Topic Consent Checkpoints

    Sensitive clinical categories - specifically Mental Health & Emotional Wellbeing, Reproductive & Maternal Health, and Substance Use & Harm Reduction - are protected by explicit interactive Consent Checkpoints. MediBot will not process or store sensitive health disclosures without your affirmative opt-in consent, and you may decline or skip any topic without losing access to general medical triage.

    3. Trauma-Informed Right to Skip & Right to Erasure

    Every clarifying clinical question includes the right to opt out ("Skip Question"). Furthermore, clicking "Clear Session Data" in the top status bar immediately purges all conversation logs, intake conditions, and consent tokens from browser memory.

    4. Voice Input & Sensor Data Processing

    When Voice Input is activated, speech-to-text transcription is processed via the browser's native Web Speech API. Low-confidence or noisy audio frames (<60% intelligibility) are automatically discarded rather than stored or guessed. Physiological vitals entered in the Sensor Check module are evaluated locally for multimodal safety verification.

    5. Contact & Grievance Redressal

    For questions regarding data privacy, accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA), or clinical governance, users may review the system documentation or clear their local session state at any time.

    Terms and Conditions of Clinical Portal Use

    Last Updated: September 26, 2026 | Clinical Governance & Medical Disclaimer

    1. Medical Disclaimer (No Physician-Patient Relationship)

    MediBot Clinical Portal is an informational health triage and clinical decision-support tool. It does not provide a definitive medical diagnosis, nor does it replace an in-person physical examination, laboratory investigation, or prescription by a licensed physician. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking emergency care because of information presented on this portal.

    2. Acute Medical Emergencies

    If you or the person you are caring for experiences chest pain, shortness of breath, sudden weakness or numbness on one side of the body, loss of consciousness, severe bleeding, snakebite, poisoning, or thoughts of self-harm, immediately call 112 (National Emergency Number in India) or proceed to the nearest hospital emergency department.

    3. Authoritative Clinical Data Sources

    Clinical guidance, triage thresholds, and public health scheme details presented by this system are synthesized from public clinical guidelines issued by:

    • World Health Organization (WHO)
    • Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
    • National Centre for Disease Control, India (NCDC)
    • Mayo Clinic Clinical Reference
    • Department of Health and Family Welfare (DoHFW)
    • Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP / Janaushadhi)
    • Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India (MoHFW)
    • National Health Mission (NHM)

    4. Medication & Generic Drug Information

    References to generic medicines from the Janaushadhi (PMBJP) catalog are provided solely for price transparency and patient education. Prescription medications (such as antihypertensives, oral antidiabetics, and antibiotics) must only be taken under the supervision and valid prescription of a registered medical practitioner.

    5. Anti-Automation-Bias & Uncertainty Flags

    Both patients and reviewing clinicians must inspect the Confidence Score and Uncertainty Flags ("What Is Unknown") attached to each response. High linguistic fluency does not equal diagnostic certainty.

    Reproductive, Menstrual & Family Planning Health Guide

    Stigma-free, non-judgmental health education with non-prescription guidance and doctor referral.

    1. "Kya family planning after marriage hoti hai?" (Family Planning & Spacing)

    Family planning and reproductive health education is for every adult couple or individual before or after marriage. Healthy birth spacing (at least 24-36 months between pregnancies) protects maternal iron stores and infant health. Visit an NHM Family Planning Counselor or Gynecologist for confidential, non-judgmental guidance.

    2. Menstrual Hygiene & Cultural Dietary Myths

    Menstruation is a natural physiological cycle. Eating curd, protein-rich dal, green leafy vegetables, or bathing daily during periods does not harm fertility. Change clean pads every 4-6 hours and use a warm water bag for cramps.

    3. PCOS & Irregular Cycle Lifestyle Care (Non-Prescription)

    30-45 minutes of daily brisk walking, reducing refined sugar/maida, adequate sleep (7-8 hours), and discussing a pelvic ultrasound + thyroid/fasting insulin check with a Gynecologist help manage PCOS naturally.

    4. Maternal Red Flags & Free JSSK Government Care

    During pregnancy, severe headache, blurred vision, facial/hand swelling, reduced fetal movement, or bleeding require immediate hospital care. Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK) provides 100% free delivery, C-section, ultrasound, and 102/108 ambulance transport.
